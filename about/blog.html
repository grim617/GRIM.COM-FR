What does it Cost to Live?

What does it cost to live? Does it include dollar signs, expenses, and purchases? How about food, water, and necessities needed to sustain your life? If I had to choose from the two options, it would be the latter. But my own answer differs from both. The cost of living isn't anything that is external from you. Choosing to really live takes life and energy out of you, but it returns it back to you tenfold. It's like a great investment. The more you put in, the better the return. To put yourself on the line, and to wander the unknown courageously, that is living. Ultimately whether living has a cost is up to the individual to discern. I'd rather think of it as the 'gift' of living rather than the cost, that way you take no L's.

Never Assume the Worst

I would never assume the worst case scenario in any situation I find to be challenging. Face the shitty news head on, assess for a brief moment, and move forward. If you stay and assess for any longer than a moment, you will no longer have control over the decision you make next. It would be as if you relinquished your free will to your thoughts, letting them run wild, filling your psyche with scenarios that will never happen, ultimately because you are afraid of taking that first step for yourself. It may seem daunting and sometimes impossible, but you will eventually learn to let life pass through you. Be a conduit for change, creativity, love and peace. Be who you are. Don't search for anything outside of yourself.

Change Your Perspective

The snow falls and lands tenderly on the concrete floor. Powdering any and every surface, nothing will be exempt from the ice. Everything stuck in the sky will eventually find its way down. The snow, taking form beautifully, and maintaining its geometry as it descends, surely to melt away. It makes its presence ever known, multiplying itself, often falling heavier at certain points of the day. How could I see amongst the flurry of nonsense? Looking ahead, past the storm that beckons before me, it appears rather brash and untamable. But, when you bring your focus to the snowflakes themselves, you’ll begin to see their preternatural shapes intuitively as they fall. You switched your perspective on how you interpret the severity of the storm. Once an unbearable showcase of mother nature’s power, turned to a showcase of her blessings among man, there to remind them of her presence, and the divine laws.

Why Should I?

Why should I blame you for your faults? Was it up to you to choose where you fall? No, but with your decisions you make the call. You chose what to say, and how to say it. In case you haven't noticed, it was you who made the game, and picked out whom you wanted to play it. So move the pieces around, change what you see fit to reach that which you desire. Keep your eye ever on the star, careful not to drown in the mire.

Rest in Peace Fofa

My favorite dog Fofa passed away in my bed this morning at around 6:22 am. She had a heart disease that caused fluid build up in her lungs, and irregular breathing. It's like her symptoms worsened overnight. One day she was excited, eager and lively, and the next she was fatigued, drained, and bed-ridden. In her last waking hours she fought to stay alive. As I stayed with her I wondered what she was clinging on to, was it food? Me? Life itself? I'll never know. She did well though, she was tough, and tried her best. She took about 3 long deep breaths before she passed over. Fofa was our first dog, born in Cape Verde, and brought to America. She taught me lessons on life, and showed me how to be a kinder, more gentle, being. She was the goat. Love you Fofa - Ni.
