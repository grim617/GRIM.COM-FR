Hello there

Welcome to my website, this is a place on the internet where you will find my ideas regarding life, work, meaning, etc.

My name is Nisaiah (Ny-Zay-Uh) Barbosa, I was born and raised in Boston Massachusettes with cultural roots to the islands of Cape Verde. I am currently in my junior year at Northeastern studying Business and Design. College wasn't on my to-do list but I found myself here regardless! On this website I put more emphasis on my writing to put who I am into perspective for those who don't know me personally.

If you like the way this website looks, you should fasho make your own! I thought it was hard but all it takes is some time allocation and effort. As does everything in life, so get yo ass up and get after it!

How can anyone make anything worth while? By trying of course! If you never start you'll never know what you could accomplish.

If I could leave one piece of advice for the world before I perish... It would be to be as open-minded as possible. Don't limit yourself to one way of doing things just because it's the only way you've ever known. Embrace the unknown and reach out to grasp everything you desire, that is what you were brought here for after all.

With that being said, thank you for visiting. I plan to upgrade this site as I live on and change. I hope you're doing well, keep striving, and be great.
